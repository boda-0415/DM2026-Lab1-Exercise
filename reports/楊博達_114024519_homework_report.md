1. Dataset Overview and Data Quality
This analysis applied an agentic data mining workflow to the Reddit Stock Sentiment dataset, containing 847 Reddit posts classified into three sentiment categories: negative (-1.0), neutral (0.0), and positive (1.0). The dataset contained 315 negative, 423 neutral, and 109 positive documents, respectively. Initial data inspection (load_dataset_1, inspect_2, missing_3, and duplicates_4) identified no missing text values but detected 34 duplicate text records, accounting for approximately 4.01% of the dataset. These duplicates were retained for subsequent analysis. Descriptive analysis (describe_5) showed that document lengths varied considerably, with a mean of approximately 148.68 characters, a median of 79 characters, and a standard deviation of 240.70 characters. The maximum document length reached 2,753 characters, indicating a right-skewed distribution with several unusually long posts. The sentiment distribution was also imbalanced, with neutral posts representing the largest category and positive posts the smallest.


2. Document-Term Matrix and Feature Filtering
A document-term matrix (DTM) was constructed (build_dtm_6) with 847 documents and 4,346 terms. The matrix contained 17,193 nonzero entries and was highly sparse, with approximately 99.53% of its entries equal to zero. Term frequency analysis (term_frequency_7) showed that common English words dominated the vocabulary, including the (920 occurrences), to (566), and and (488), while stock-related terms such as market (111) and stock (51) appeared less frequently. A DTM heatmap (dtm_heatmap_8) further illustrated the sparsity of the term-frequency representation. Variance filtering (variance_filter_9) using a threshold of 0.01 retained 506 terms and removed 3,840 low-variance terms. Pearson correlation filtering was then performed separately for positive (pearson_filter_10) and negative (pearson_filter_11) sentiment using a one-vs-rest approach and a threshold of 0.1, retaining 41 and 23 terms, respectively. Positively associated terms for the positive category included huge, nice, and technology, while terms associated with the negative category included trump, china, economy, and trade. These results demonstrate how feature filtering can reduce dimensionality and identify sentiment-associated vocabulary, although the presence of common words suggests that additional text preprocessing could improve interpretability.


3. Feature Relationships and Frequent Pattern Mining
Feature correlation analysis (feature_corr_12) examined relationships among frequently occurring terms and generated a corresponding heatmap. Several common English words exhibited relatively strong positive correlations, including the and in (0.6943), the and of (0.6727), and the and and (0.6623). The terms market and will also showed a positive correlation of approximately 0.5886. These relationships suggest that common vocabulary and document-level word frequencies may influence the observed correlation structure. Frequent pattern mining was performed on the negative sentiment category using FP-Growth and variance-based filtering. With a minimum support count of 10 (mine_patterns_14), no frequent itemsets were identified. Reducing the minimum support count to 3 (mine_patterns_15) produced 226 frequent itemsets, as verified from the stored results. Frequently occurring singleton patterns included buying, country, feel, know, support, trust, war, and worse, each with a support count of 8. These findings demonstrate that frequent pattern discovery is sensitive to the minimum support threshold, while the dominance of single-term patterns limits conclusions about meaningful multi-word associations.


4. Dimensionality Reduction and Label Binarization
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was performed with two components (dim_red_16) on a feature matrix containing 847 documents and 1,777 terms. The resulting coordinate matrix contained 847 observations and two dimensions, preserving all documents. The first principal component explained approximately 27.00% of the variance, while the second explained 4.99%, giving a combined explained variance ratio of 31.99%. This indicates that the two-dimensional representation captured approximately one-third of the variance in the PCA input matrix, while substantial information remained in higher dimensions. The PCA input contained 1,777 features, differing from both the original 4,346-term DTM and the 506 features retained by the earlier variance filter; therefore, these results should be interpreted as analyses of different feature representations. Label binarization (binarize_labels_17) successfully converted the three sentiment categories into a one-hot encoded matrix of shape 847 by 3, with the class order -1.0, 0.0, and 1.0.


5. Document Similarity and Conclusion
Finally, cosine similarity was calculated between documents 0 and 1 (cosine_18) using their DTM term-frequency representations. The resulting similarity score was 0.0000, indicating that the two documents had no overlapping nonzero vocabulary features in the representation used. The first document was a very short comment, while the second discussed corporate involvement in politics. Although their term-frequency vectors were orthogonal, this result does not necessarily imply different sentiment labels, since lexical similarity and sentiment similarity measure different aspects of text. Overall, the workflow demonstrated a comprehensive approach to Reddit sentiment data exploration, feature selection, correlation analysis, frequent pattern mining, dimensionality reduction, and document similarity. Important limitations include the dominance of stop words, the highly sparse DTM, imbalanced sentiment categories, retained duplicate records, and differences in feature representations across analytical steps. Future improvements could include more extensive text preprocessing, TF-IDF weighting, and supervised sentiment classification to evaluate the predictive usefulness of the selected features.
